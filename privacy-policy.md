# Privacy Policy — Dash Legacy: City Run

**Last updated:** [23-9-2026]

This policy explains how Dash Legacy: City Run ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

## The short version

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no data sent to any server. Everything the App needs to run stays on your device.

## What the App stores, and where

- **Game progress and settings** (best distance, in-game currency, unlocked shop items, DMG Mode toggle, easy mode, car skin choice) are saved locally on your device using Apple's standard `UserDefaults` storage.
- This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App.

## Sharing feature

The App includes an optional "Share" button that lets you post a screenshot of your run using Apple's built-in share sheet. If you choose to share to another app (Messages, Instagram, etc.), that content and any data associated with it is governed by that third-party app's own privacy policy — we have no access to it and no involvement beyond handing your device's share sheet the image.

## Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data is collected for any purpose by us or by any partner.

## Children's privacy

Because the App collects no personal data from any user, this applies equally to children. The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

## Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library. Audio playback uses the device's standard audio session and does not access the microphone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future update adds analytics, ads, or account features — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
